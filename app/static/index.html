Everything you need to know about HL7 Inspector


      

        

          
📥

          
Input

          
Paste HL7 v2 messages directly, upload .hl7 or .txt files, or load built-in samples. Custom delimiters from
            MSH-1 and MSH-2 are honored.

        

        

          
🔍

          
Parse

          
Segment-by-segment inspection with field positions, repetitions, components, and subcomponents preserved
            exactly as received.

        

        

          
✅

          
Validate

          
Structural and profile-lite checks producing ERROR / WARNING / INFO results with field locations and
            suggested corrections.

        

        

          
🔄

          
Convert

          
Generate generic JSON, well-formed XML, FHIR R4 Bundles (Patient, Encounter, Observation), and HL7
            acknowledgements (AA / AE / AR).
